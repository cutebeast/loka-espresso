Golden rules: ① One redemption per order (voucher OR reward, every channel, both directions). ② Redemptions are order-tied — scanning only validates; released on cancel. ③ Error paths never consume. ④ Points follow the money — credit on capture, pro-rata clawback on refunds. ⑤ Captured money is never auto-cancelled. ⑥ Guests earn nothing.
