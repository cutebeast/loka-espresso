# Help Center Articles

Manage the in-app help content shown to admin, staff, and customer users.

## What you can do

- Create, edit, and deactivate help articles.
- Target articles to a specific audience: admin, staff, or customer.
- Organize articles by category and set display order.

## Writing effective help articles

- Use clear headings and short paragraphs.
- Include tables or lists for step-by-step instructions.
- Keep the tone consistent with your brand voice.

Articles are rendered with markdown support, including tables, so you can present structured guidance clearly.
