# Admin Profile

Manage your own account settings, password, and preferences.

## What you can do

- Update your display name and contact details.
- Change your password.
- Review your role and store assignments.

## Changing your password

1. Open the profile menu and select **Profile**.
2. Enter your current password and the new password twice.
3. Save. You will be logged out on other devices and asked to log in again.

For security, use a strong password and enable two-factor authentication when available.
