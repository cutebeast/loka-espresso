# Equipment Maintenance

Track restaurant equipment, schedule preventive maintenance, and log breakdowns or repairs.

## What you can do

- View all equipment registered for a store.
- Record maintenance reports and repair notes.
- Mark equipment as operational, under maintenance, or out of service.

## Adding equipment

1. Go to **Store Ops > Equipment**.
2. Click **New Equipment**.
3. Enter the name, model/serial number, location, and responsible staff.
4. Save.

## Maintenance reports

Use the **Reports** tab to log:

- Issue description
- Maintenance date
- Cost and parts replaced
- Next scheduled service

Keeping reports up to date helps avoid unexpected downtime and supports warranty claims.
