# Admin Users & Roles

Control who can access the Admin Portal and what they are allowed to do.

## What you can do

- Invite or deactivate admin accounts.
- Assign roles such as system admin, store manager, regional manager, or readonly analyst.
- Review role permissions and store assignments.

## Built-in roles

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| system_admin | Full access to all stores and settings |
| store_manager | Assigned stores only; no Settings, Translations, or Audit Log |
| regional_manager | All stores; multi-store oversight |
| readonly_analyst | All stores; view only |

## Best practices

- Follow the principle of least privilege: give users only the access they need.
- Deactivate accounts promptly when staff leave.
- Review the Audit Log regularly for sensitive actions.
