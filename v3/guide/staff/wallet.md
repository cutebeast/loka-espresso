# Staff Portal: Wallet

## Page
`/wallet`

## Purpose
Check customer wallet balances and process wallet top-ups.

## Check a customer wallet

1. Open **Wallet**.
2. Search by customer phone number or scan their QR code.
3. View current balance and recent transactions.

## Top up a wallet

1. Open **Wallet**.
2. Search for the customer.
3. Tap **Top Up**.
4. Enter amount.
5. Select payment method (cash or card/gateway).
6. Complete payment.
7. Balance updates immediately.

## Wallet transactions

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| credit | Money added to wallet. |
| debit | Money spent from wallet. |
| refund | Refund credited to wallet. |

## Limitations
- Wallet top-ups via card/gateway may redirect to a payment provider.
- Maximum top-up amount is configured per store.
