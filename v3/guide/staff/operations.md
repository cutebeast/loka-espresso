# Staff Portal: Operations

## Pages
- `/inventory` — Inventory Update
- `/equipment` — Equipment Check
- `/garbage` — Garbage Disposal Log
- `/grease-trap` — Grease Trap Log
- `/wastage` — Wastage Report

## Inventory (`/inventory`)

### Purpose
Update stock levels for inventory items.

### Update stock
1. Open **Inventory**.
2. Select the inventory item.
3. Enter the new quantity or adjustment.
4. Select reason.
5. Save.

### When to use
- After a physical stock count.
- When receiving stock without a purchase order.
- When correcting a discrepancy.

## Equipment (`/equipment`)

### Purpose
Check equipment condition and report maintenance issues.

### Submit an equipment check
1. Open **Equipment**.
2. Select the equipment item.
3. Choose condition:
   - Operational — Checked OK
   - Maintenance Needed
   - Broken / Not Working
4. Add notes.
5. Upload photos if needed.
6. Save.

## Garbage (`/garbage`)

### Purpose
Log garbage disposal completion.

### Submit
1. Open **Garbage**.
2. Add notes.
3. Upload photos confirming disposal.
4. Save.

## Grease Trap (`/grease-trap`)

### Purpose
Log grease trap cleaning.

### Submit
1. Open **Grease Trap**.
2. Upload before-cleaning photos.
3. Upload after-cleaning photos.
4. Add notes.
5. Save.

## Wastage (`/wastage`)

### Purpose
Report wasted inventory.

### Submit
1. Open **Wastage**.
2. Select the inventory item.
3. Enter quantity wasted.
4. Select reason (expired, spoiled, damaged, etc.).
5. Add notes.
6. Save.

## Who can access

These pages are available to `shift_supervisor`, `store_manager`, `kitchen_staff`, and admin users.
