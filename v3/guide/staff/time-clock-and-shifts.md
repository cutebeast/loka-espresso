# Staff Portal: Time Clock & Shifts

## Pages
- `/time-clock` — Time Clock
- `/shifts` — My Shifts

## Time Clock (`/time-clock`)

### Purpose
Record attendance: clock in, start break, end break, and clock out.

### Clock in
1. Tap **Time Clock**.
2. Tap **Clock In**.
3. Your start time is recorded.

### Start / end break
1. Open **Time Clock**.
2. Tap **Start Break** when leaving for a break.
3. Tap **End Break** when returning.

### Clock out
1. Open **Time Clock**.
2. Tap **Clock Out**.
3. Your end time is recorded.

### Current status
The page shows your current clock status and the duration of your current shift.

### Geolocation
If enabled, your location may be captured when clocking in/out.

## Shifts (`/shifts`)

### Purpose
View your upcoming shifts.

### What you see
- Shift date
- Start and end time
- Store
- Role

### View range
The page shows shifts for the next 30 days by default.

## Manager view

Managers and shift supervisors manage shift templates and assignments in the Admin Portal under **Staff → Shifts**.
