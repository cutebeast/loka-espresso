# Customer PWA: Getting Started

## Installing the PWA

The Loka Espresso Customer PWA works best when added to your home screen.

### Android (Chrome)
1. Open `app.loyaltysystem.uk`.
2. Tap the menu (three dots).
3. Tap **Add to Home screen**.
4. Confirm.

### iOS (Safari)
1. Open `app.loyaltysystem.uk`.
2. Tap the share icon.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm.

## Creating an account

1. Open the PWA.
2. Tap **Sign Up**.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Enter the OTP sent to your phone.
5. Complete your profile (name, email optional).
6. Tap **Done**.

## Logging in

1. Open the PWA.
2. Tap **Log In**.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Enter the OTP.
5. You are logged in.

## Selecting a store

If the brand has multiple stores:

1. The store picker appears on first launch.
2. Select the store you want to order from.
3. You can change store later in **Profile → Settings**.

## Home screen

The home page shows:
- Featured promotions and banners.
- Quick category filters.
- Recommended items.
- Loyalty tier and points summary.

## Navigation

| Icon | Page |
|------|------|
| Home | Home feed and featured items. |
| Menu | Full menu browse. |
| Cart | Current cart. |
| Orders | Order history and active orders. |
| Profile | Account, wallet, rewards, settings. |

## Push notifications

Allow notifications to receive updates about:
- Order status changes
- Promotions
- Loyalty rewards

## Help

Tap **Profile → Help & Support** for FAQ and contact options.
