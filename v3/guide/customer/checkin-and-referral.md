# Customer PWA: Check-in & Referral

## Pages
- `checkin` — Daily Check-in
- `referral` — Referral Program

## Check-in page

### Purpose
Earn bonus loyalty points by checking in daily.

### How to check in
1. Open **Check-in**.
2. Tap the **Check In** button.
3. Points are added to your loyalty account.

### Streak bonuses
Check in on consecutive days to earn streak bonuses. The longer the streak, the bigger the bonus.

### Rules
- One check-in per day.
- Streak resets if you miss a day.
- Bonus points are configured by the store.

## Referral page

### Purpose
Invite friends and earn rewards when they sign up and place their first order.

### How it works
1. Open **Referral**.
2. Copy your unique referral code or share link.
3. Share via messaging apps, email, or social media.
4. When a friend signs up with your code, both of you receive a reward.

### Track referrals
The page shows:
- Total referrals sent
- Successful sign-ups
- Rewards earned

### Referral rewards
Rewards are configured by the store and may include:
- Wallet credit
- Voucher
- Bonus points
