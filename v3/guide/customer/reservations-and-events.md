# Customer PWA: Reservations & Events

## Pages
- `reservations` — Reservations (table bookings)
- `events` — Events (RSVP to what's on)
- `workshops` — Workshops (book paid classes and experiences)

## Three separate things

| | What it is | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| Table reservation | A table held for you on a date and time | **Reservations** |
| Event | A happening at the café you RSVP to (free or invite-only) | **Events** |
| Workshop / experience | A paid class with limited seats (e.g. the Coffee Omakase Experience) | **Workshops** |

Bookings don't cross over: a table reservation doesn't include a workshop seat, and a workshop booking doesn't reserve you a table. Check each section separately.

## Reservations page

Sign in to book or view reservations.

### Book a table
1. Open **Reservations** and tap **+**.
2. Pick the store, date, and time.
3. Choose the party size (1–20 guests).
4. Add any special requests (e.g. high chair, window seat) — optional.
5. Tap **Confirm Booking**.

Your booking starts as **Pending** until the store confirms it. You'll see the status on the card. You can't book a date in the past.

### View your reservations
Open **Reservations** to see all your bookings. Each card shows the store name and address, the date and time, party size, your special requests, and a colored status pill. Newest bookings appear first.

### Reservation statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Sent to the store — waiting for confirmation. |
| Confirmed | The store has confirmed your table. |
| Seated | You've arrived and been seated. |
| Completed | Your visit is finished. |
| No Show | The booking passed without you arriving. |
| Cancelled | Cancelled by you or the store. |

### Cancel a reservation
1. Open **Reservations** and find the booking.
2. Tap the cancel (trash) icon on the card — shown while the booking is still **Pending** or **Confirmed**.
3. Confirm the cancellation.

Plans change? Cancel as early as you can so the table frees up for other guests.

## Events page

### What you see
Upcoming events at LOKA Espresso. You can browse without signing in. Each card shows the date, location, and a tag:

| Tag | Meaning |
|-----|---------|
| RSVP count (e.g. 12/30) | Open event — anyone can join until it fills. |
| Invite Only | Only invited guests can join. |
| Paid Event | A ticketed session — book it through Workshops instead. |

### RSVP to an event
1. Open **Events** and tap an event.
2. Check the date, time, location, and how many seats are left.
3. Tap **RSVP** (sign in if prompted).
4. The button changes to **You're going!** — your spot is held.

The button is disabled when the event is full or if you've already RSVP'd. Invite-only events stay locked unless the café has invited your account. To cancel an RSVP, please contact the store.

### Paid events
Paid events have a booking button (e.g. **Book Now**) that takes you to the workshop booking flow below.

## Workshops page

### What you see
Paid classes and experiences — like the Coffee Omakase Experience — with a description, the price, and the next few sessions. Each session chip shows the date, start time, location, and how many seats are left (or **Full**). You can browse without signing in; you'll sign in when you check out.

### Book a workshop
1. Open **Workshops** (or tap the booking button on a paid event).
2. Tap **Book Now** on the class you want.
3. Pick a session — you must choose a date before the class can be added to your cart.
4. Confirm to add it to your cart, then check out and pay in the app (wallet balance or online payment).
5. Your seat is reserved when you place the order, and the booking is confirmed once payment goes through.

If a session shows **Full**, pick another date. If a session sells out while you're checking out, the app will tell you — go back and choose a different session.

### Where's my booking?
Workshop bookings appear with your orders: open **Orders** to see the booking and payment. They are not listed under Reservations.

## Tips

- Book early for weekends — both tables and workshop seats go fast.
- Running late for a reservation? Call the store so they don't mark it as a no-show.
- Workshop seats are limited and a session can sell out while you're deciding — book and pay in one go.
