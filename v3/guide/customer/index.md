# Customer PWA Guide

## What the Customer PWA is for

The Customer PWA is the customer-facing app for browsing the menu, placing orders, earning loyalty points, redeeming rewards, managing a profile, and paying.

## Who should use this guide

- **Customers** — end users of the PWA.
- **Support staff** — helping customers with the app.
- **Admin users** — understanding the customer journey.

## First use

1. Open the Customer PWA URL in a mobile browser.
2. Allow the prompt to **Add to Home Screen** for the best experience.
3. Register with your phone number or log in.
4. If OTP is enabled, enter the code sent to your phone.
5. Select a store (if multiple stores are available).

## Ordering journey from the customer view

1. Customer opens PWA and selects a store.
2. Browses **Menu** and taps items to add to cart.
3. Customises items with modifiers.
4. Goes to **Cart** and reviews the order.
5. Proceeds to **Checkout**.
6. Selects order type:
   - **Dine-in** — scans table QR or selects table.
   - **Takeaway** — pickup time.
   - **Delivery** — enters delivery address.
7. Applies ONE voucher or reward if available (never both).
8. Selects payment method and pays.
9. Order appears in **Orders** with live status updates.
10. Customer receives notifications when order is confirmed, preparing, and ready.

## Guest browsing

Guests (not signed in) can browse the whole app — menu, promotions, events, workshops, and store info. Placing an order requires an account (phone number + one OTP), so vouchers, rewards, points, and wallet always belong to a signed-in customer. In-store, staff can serve customers as walk-ins without an app account (no vouchers/rewards/points for walk-ins).
