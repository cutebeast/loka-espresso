# Customer PWA: Information

## Page
`information`

## Purpose
Browse informational content created by the store, such as articles, product highlights, and experiences.

## Tabs

| Tab | Content |
|-----|---------|
| Experiences | Events and experiences offered by the store. |
| Products | Featured product cards. |
| Articles | Info cards and blog-style content. |

## Read an article
1. Open **Information**.
2. Select a tab.
3. Tap a card or article.
4. Read the full content.
